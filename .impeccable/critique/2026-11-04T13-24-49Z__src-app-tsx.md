#### Overall Impression
A professionally crafted, clinically trustworthy veterinary hospital experience. The medical taxonomy is stellar. The primary vulnerability is emergency cognitive conflict: a panicked owner at 2:00 AM faces 3 competing CTAs in the Hero and an online booking form that doesn't immediately intercept life-threatening crises.
